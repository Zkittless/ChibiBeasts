CHIBIBEASTS — GAMEPLAY REVIVAL

Starter awakenings
Prismite -> Prismara: crystal serval, shard mantle and three faceted tails.
Twine -> Timestitch: clockwork jerboa, spring legs, spool back and ribbon tail.
Gloop -> Nebuloom: jelly salamander, four flippers, star fins and crescent tail.
Barkley -> Rootwarden: forest drake, leaf wings, wooden antlers and eggshell chestplate.

All four have distinct ImageGen artwork and new passives. Reach beast level 15,
bond 30 and happiness 70; check /bond, then /evolve. No catalyst is required.
Nickname, level, EXP, training, disposition, earned stat boosts, Ambrosia bonus,
equipment and party assignments are preserved.

Changes addressing the game review
1. All ten campaign chapters connect. Material events, snapshot objectives,
   chapter links and legacy chapter-five continuation now work. Claims pay once.
2. Shard and guild purchases grant real effects. Items validate their target
   before consumption. Time skips move both tending and hatching schedules.
3. All duel modes share one combat engine. Armor, evasion, guard and shields
   apply before HP loss. An enemy ultimate only spends its own charge.
4. Runes can be crafted and equipped. Every crafting material has a real route.
   Ancient loot is defined and routed to the right inventory. Evolution-only
   forms cannot hatch randomly. All twelve perks have normal unlock conditions.
5. Catch, hatch, battle, training and other events feed a shared progression
   path. Batch actions count correctly. Daily sets stay stable and gate raid
   objectives. Titles require earned achievements, collections or story choices.
6. The three move roles now differ: mana-building attack, typed or healing skill,
   and guard/focus. Ranked uses the interactive duel rules and dated seasons.
7. Starters use the same EXP curve as other companions and have bond awakenings.
8. Exploration and challenges use chosen destinations and regional species.
   Region gates are 1/3/5/8/10. Relic searches finish after three relevant visits.
9. Raids have lobbies, three companions per trainer, swapping, tending allies
   and six boss responses. Active support earns rewards. Catch progress
   guarantees a companion after eight Corrupted or twelve Ancient clears of
   that species. Interrupted paid summons are refunded once on restart.
10. The finale offers care, curiosity or courage. Each has a scene and title,
    restores the hollow, and leaves persistent post-story NPC dialogue.
11. Sable's clasp reaches Maren, Maren and Orren reunite, and the hatched World
    Tree Seed helps resolve the Fraying. Corrupted raid victories leave the
    creatures alive. Lore distinguishes the First Voices, Divine Collections,
    Altered companions, Corrupted encounters and Ancient encounters.
12. /guide lists real registered commands and current destinations. Item,
    Sanctuary and perk descriptions were brought into line with their effects.

Additional fixes
Concurrent crafting, claims, item use, equipment changes, perk equipping and
guild creation validate fresh state. Replacing armor preserves other copies.
KO timers do not restart on every boss action, and expired companions recover.
Achievement pages fit Discord embed limits. Windows JSON reads use UTF-8.
Retired duplicate combat and raid implementations were removed.

Verification
55 automated tests passed, with zero failures or errors. Python compilation
passed. All 16 Discord extensions loaded without login. All 86 artwork hashes
match the manifest. The tests use disposable databases and mocked interactions.
See docs/gameplay-validation.json and tests/test_gameplay.py for details.

Review and deployment
The update is saved on the local revamp/gameplay-completion branch. No remote
push or deployment was performed. The existing live player database was not
used for testing. Back up your deployment database before upgrading; startup
migrations preserve existing accounts and collections. Deploy the code and
PNG artwork together, then sync commands and playtest on a Discord server.
Permissions, multi-player timing and long-term balance need that live test.

The ZIP contains the bot, data, all 86 sprites, tests, exact art prompts, this
report and lightweight local previews. Large standalone gallery exports and
the historical five-design comparison are kept in the repository; rebuild
them with the gallery scripts if needed. Player databases, secrets, caches,
local tools and nested ZIPs are excluded.
