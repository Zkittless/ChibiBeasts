Beyond the art

A campaign with an ending.

Ten connected chapters now resolve the clasp, the reunion, the Fraying and the World Tree Seed. Choose care, curiosity or courage at the hollow, with a lasting scene and title.

More decisions in every fight.

Reliable attacks restore mana, typed skills change the battle, and guarding prepares your next strike. Gear, bonds and passives share the same rules in wild battles, spars, PvP and ranked. Raids add ally tending, swapping and six boss-specific Weave responses; support earns rewards too.

Rewards that reach the player.

Working shard and guild purchases, usable consumables, crafting for runes, real forging-material routes, reachable perks and reliable story claims. Evolutions preserve nickname, training, earned stat boosts, gear and party assignments.
