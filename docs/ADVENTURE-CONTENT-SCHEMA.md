# Authored adventure content contract

Each `data/adventures/<biome>.json` contains `version: 1`, its stable `biome` ID, and an `encounters` array. IDs are permanent and prefixed with that biome. Each biome needs **20 distinct one-time encounter roots**, including at least three connected locations/story chains with 3–5 nodes each, plus **six explicitly repeatable wilderness encounters**. Nodes within one location are not counted as separate encounter roots. No runtime AI or generated synonym variants.

Encounter fields: `id`, `title`, `kind`, `repeatable` (boolean), `entry` (node ID), `tags` (array), `nodes` (object keyed by node ID). Optional `requires_flags` and `forbid_flags` arrays control later discoveries. Kinds: `discovery`, `ruin`, `traveller`, `shrine`, `wild`, `pack`, `hazard`, `camp`, `legendary`. A root's first node must offer a meaningful safe/noncombat option. Never require a species, item purchase, element or timed response to finish.

Node fields: `body` (roughly 35–80 concrete words), `choices` (2–3). Choice fields: permanent `id`, `label` (under 70 characters), `description` (under 100 characters, honest mechanical consequence), `outcome` (one or two authored sentences), `effects` (object), and optional `next` (another node key). Missing `next` completes this encounter. Optional choice `requires_flags` / `forbid_flags` constrain world-state branches; at least one safe choice must always be available. Flags must use permanent biome-prefixed names. Choices may `set_flags` / `clear_flags` arrays inside effects, connecting a discovery to another root. No revisiting node cycles.

Supported effects (only these): `attack_boon` or `defense_boon` (5 or 10 percent, temporary team-wide, combined cap 20% per stat), `heal` (5 or 10 percent surviving-team maximum HP, bounded by the run's recovery budget), `hp_cost` (3 or 5 percent, cannot cause a knockout), `bonus` (2 or 5 percent of the single run reward, capped 25%), `bond` (1), `study` (1, once-per-run research allowance), `material` (true, selects/replaces the run's one material; no extra bundle), `battle` (`single`, `pack2`, `pack3`, or `rare`), `set_flags` / `clear_flags` (arrays). Do not invent currencies, inventory rewards, permanent buffs, catches, revives, rarity guarantees or arbitrary numerical fields. Every choice must have a meaningful effect, a different next node, or a persistent consequence; do not pad choices with equivalent outcomes.

For `material: true`, departure freezes the rolled material (including a current quest material) plus up to two other existing regional materials of the **same rarity**. When at least two are available, resolving that authored choice opens a saved material picker over the already-advanced next scene. Selecting a named material replaces the one return bundle and restores that scene; it adds no journey step, rarity roll or item grant. Returning early keeps the currently selected material. If the region has no same-rarity alternative, the choice instead grants up to **+2% bundle bonus**, sharing the existing 25% run cap, and keeps its saved material. The displayed consequence reports this bounded fallback and any HP cost. Battle choices defer this picker or bonus until victory; defeat and retreat grant neither. An optional nest egg still replaces the selected material rather than adding a second bundle.

A battle effect announces entry and intent, never claims victory before combat. Its `next` scene is shown only after combat resolves. Rare/legendary scenes can describe a distant or peaceful meeting; rarity and capture availability are controlled by the shared destination/encounter rules, not by prose or these effects. All companion names must exist in `data/beasts.json`; no new species. Avoid fights with story-only, Ancient, Corrupted or developer entities. Friendly celestial visitors may remain unnamed.

Write each biome as a distinct place with its own people, ecology, history, stakes and sensory details. Use familiar concrete language. Choices should involve competing benefits, curiosity, trust or stewardship, rather than a repeated safe/risky/help menu. No identical bodies/outcomes across biomes or stories. Completed one-time encounters never restart for that trainer; unfinished chains resume their next unresolved node. Repeatable events are labelled familiar territory and have within-run/recent-history exclusions. Finite content exhaustion must be visible and honest.

Catalog material families, trainer gates and levels remain from `utils/habitats.py`; item/economy and canon lore are authoritative. Content must pass schema, ID/edge/flag, reachability, nonempty choice and outcome/effect validation, plus an independent editorial pass.
