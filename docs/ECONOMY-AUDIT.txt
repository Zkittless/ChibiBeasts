OUTCOME

Reviewed all 52 offers across gold, shard and guild shops, all
29 crafting recipes (16 armor, 10 runes, 3 evolution catalysts),
and all 21 materials. Every recipe has a reachable source. All materials
now have at least one crafting use. No gold offers are free. No tested egg
bonus combination has positive expected release resale. The optimistic
repeatable cooldown-reset loop also loses gold at every modeled level.

Basic gear now takes about 8-11 explorations on average, with no Guild Forge,
instead of 20-35 in the original model. The six level-one basics average
1.1-1.4 days at eight explorations per day, or roughly 2.1-2.8 at four.
This is a seeded resource model, not measured player retention or live timing.

WHY THE OLD ECONOMY NEEDED CHANGES

1. Three guaranteed-Divine incubators were sold for zero gold. Their prices
   are now 80,000g with a trainer-level-25 purchase gate. Legendary incubation
   cost 50,000g despite Epic incubators offering much cheaper broad Divine
   chances. Legendary now costs 24,000g, keeping a stronger rarity floor and
   curated pools as its reasons to buy. Divine offers a guaranteed focused
   collection. The campaign's World-Tree Seed remains outside the shop.

2. Stardust/Observatory modifiers could put expensive rarity outcomes into
   200g Common Eggs. There were 11 profitable
   combinations in the original ledger. Bonuses now improve only rarity tiers
   already offered by an instant egg. Glimmering Fortune keeps its 0.05%
   Divine pre-roll. All 64 instant-egg modifier combinations plus 18 incubators
   have negative expected release profit. Fortune occurs before the Altered
   roll; the baseline analytic ledger was corrected for that ordering.

3. Basic crafting depended on winning/catching an encounter, then receiving
   the right material. Losing could prevent the upgrades needed to win later.
   Every completed exploration now grants one resource, including quiet trips
   and escapes. Regional weights favor basic resources, with extra weight
   from matching species/elements. Woods ponds can supply Kelpie Scales.
   Several basic recipes need fewer units, while higher tiers retain their
   larger ingredient and raid requirements. There is no flat material shop.

4. Raids awarded personal guild_tokens, but /guild_shop spent guild_tickets.
   New raid rewards pay spendable Guild Tickets. A revision-keyed migration
   converts saved personal tokens into tickets once and clears that legacy
   balance. The shared guild bank is separate and is not converted.

5. Guild EXP needed 500 x current level; it now needs 250 x current level.
   At 115 average EXP per daily contribution, level five requires about 22
   total contributions: 7.25 days for three contributors or 2.17 for ten.
   These are means for a fixed roster contributing daily, not guarantees.
   Contribution claims now run in a writer transaction, so overlapping calls
   cannot bypass the 24-hour timer or award the same day's rewards twice.

6. Training scaled from 50g to 20,000g per session for small stat changes.
   Costs now range from 50g to 1,000g; new HP sessions grant three times the
   normal point gain. Existing sessions, counters and earned stats are kept.
   Training still has per-stat lifetime caps, and rarer companions retain
   larger point gains. No inventory or companion reset is required.

7. Carrots cost 40g while their EXP scaled indefinitely with trainer level.
   They now cost 150g and give 25 + 5 x trainer level, capped at trainer 30
   (175 EXP). At trainer 50 the old 525 EXP per carrot has become 175.
   Battles remain the normal free growth route; food helps a chosen companion
   catch up. Star-Candy costs 1,500g, with 30 tracked uses per companion.
   The limit and gains survive evolution. Legacy bonuses stay; old treat
   counts were never recorded, so legacy counters start at zero.

8. Temporary defense/speed items could multiply current stats repeatedly in
   one fight. One item of each type can now be queued/applied per battle.
   A duplicate attempt preserves the item, including when the first boost
   was queued before entering combat. Permanent stat treats require finishing
   the current fight. Ambrosia retains its existing 25% damage cap.

9. Selling a 75,000g Genesis Fruit returned 26,250g. Ancient materials sold
   for 3,000g each. Together they dwarfed normal rewards and most useful sinks.
   Genesis Fruit's NPC resale is now 1,500g, Ambrosia 500g, Star-Candy 250g.
   Material resale by rarity is 20/40/80/180/400/800g. The merchant's sale
   bonus still applies. Loot remains more valuable for use/forging than
   liquidation; bosses remain a rewarding way to earn resources.

   Example, level-10 Ancient Genesis, selling every guaranteed/random drop:
   Before expected gross: 18,253.33g
   After expected gross:  3,403.33g
   Direct gold is still 500g and base shards still 8. The difference comes
   from NPC sale values, not deleting drops. The old ledger's personal token
   column explicitly records legacy tokens, which were not Guild Tickets.

10. Repeatable exploration, challenge and daily gold scale through level 50.
    EXP/levels still progress, and one-time level milestones remain. Chrono-
    Biscuits now cost 3,500g. Even an optimistic loop that wins and releases
    every creature, selects the best habitats/rarity bias, and receives the
    most valuable possible regional material has negative expected profit.
    Its best case is -126.45g per reset after merchant discount. Lucky
    individual outcomes can profit; this is an expected-value check. Finite
    compass/seal charges and daily/story rewards are excluded from a
    repeatable loop because using them consumes another limited resource.

11. A gold Loom Catalyst could halve 96 remaining hours for 800g, overshadowing
    the six-hour shard/ticket skips. It now moves the schedule two hours closer
    for 800g. A six-hour Loom Fragment costs 5 shards (two per week), or
    30 Guild Tickets at guild level five. Boosts still move tending and hatch
    deadlines together, and the existing free weekly perk skip remains.

12. Prism Keys cost 15 shards instead of 30. Their 30% Divine odds and the
    Divine Compass's 20% odds now exclude empty rarity pools before setting
    the percentage. An Ancient Compass waits if a habitat is already entirely
    Rare or higher. All three Ancient summons cost 60 shards, one of each per
    week, at trainer level 10. These same-tier bosses no longer have arbitrary
    60/80/100 pricing. Corrupted raid drops remain an alternate summon route.

13. Recipes now show real effects, crafting levels, actual discounted costs
    and specific material sources. Forge saves one per ingredient stack,
    minimum one; the old 10% claim was misleading for tiny integer recipes.
    Store checkout reads the loaded catalog and fresh wallet/level/perks,
    rather than trusting a price captured by an old message. Autocomplete
    agrees with crafting gates and Forge costs. Rune prices that were never
    actually offered in the shop were removed; all ten runes are craftable.

14. Fenrir Fur had no recipe use. Abyssal Shroud now needs three Kraken Ink
    and two Fenrir Fur. Ouroboros Ring needs three Chronos Gear, two Creator
    Tears and four Kraken Ink, rather than a one-Ancient-clear shortcut.
    Its level-10 guaranteed route is two Chronos plus two Corrupted Leviathan
    clears; higher raid tiers or Forge can reduce this. Gear effects retain
    their existing combat roles. The prior 5-8 minute raid budget is retained.

15. Exploration applied Arcane Library twice. It now receives the advertised
    15% trainer EXP bonus once. Advanced companion rarities were treated as
    Common for challenge encounter bias; they now use the top standard tier.
    This improves encounter progression without adding gold beyond the cap.

Each of 29 recipes was modeled with and without Forge, 200 fixed seeds per
case: 11,600 acquisition trials before and 11,600 after. The foraging policy
chooses a destination for the slowest missing ingredient. It assumes all
destinations are available; the six level-one basics have their material
sources in Woods. The first common kit therefore does not require raiding.

Baseline: 5% quiet trips; otherwise affinity or 75% modeled encounter wins
before a material could drop. Current: exactly one material per completed
exploration. Rare materials retain randomness. Recipe CSVs include P90,
minimum source/craft levels, ingredients, material sale opportunity cost,
mandatory Ancient clears, and alternate guaranteed raid plans. These are
fresh-inventory, one-recipe estimates. Carryover resources and higher-tier
raid rewards can speed subsequent crafts; failed raids can delay them.

For example, Dragon-Lord Aegis takes about 55 explorations if forged entirely
from exploration, or two level-10 Corrupted Dragon clears plus two Iron Ore.
Abyssal Shroud's guaranteed route is two Leviathan and one Fenrir clear.
Chronos Plate needs two Chronos clears plus two Papyrus. These are ingredient
routes after the player can win those raids, not promises of immediate wins.

Casual: 4 explores, 2 challenges, 1 spar, two modeled quests worth 400g.
Active: 8 explores, 4 challenges, 3 spars, three modeled quests worth 600g.
Raider: Active plus two Corrupted clears. Combat win assumption is 75%.
Levels are held fixed and companion level equals trainer level. These are
planning scenarios, not simulated new-account timelines or measured play.

Gold includes /daily and direct rewards. The separate optional sale column
uses the most valuable available exploration destination. It is not added
to the base income because selling ingredients delays crafting. Income
excludes creature release, raid loot sales, one-time milestones, story and
achievement rewards, market activity, spending and most perks. Spar access,
quest completion and real win rates vary between accounts.

At level 15, the active budget is about 2,682g/day before spending, so a
24,000g Legendary incubator is roughly nine saving days, instead of nineteen
at the old price. A 75,000g Fruit would be about 28 days under that narrow
budget; its raid drop is the practical alternate path. At level 25, 80,000g
is roughly 22 active saving days, before other sources or purchases. Endgame
guarantees remain goals, while basic gear and recovery items are earlier wins.

The base active shard budget (daily plus three spars at 75% wins) is 4.25/day,
before quests/story/raids/perks. A 60-shard summon is about fourteen such
saving days; sharing hosts and Corrupted summon drops shorten the route.
Weekly stock limits are separate from earning time. Guild Ticket purchase
tables show both contribution-only days and equivalent base raid clears.
