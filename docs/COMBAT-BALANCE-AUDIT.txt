OUTCOME

The requested raid target is 5-8 minutes for prepared, active parties. The
final fresh-seed cohort ran 576 trials across all six bosses, levels
10/15/30/50, and 1/3/6/10 trainers. It won 570/576 trials
(99.0%). Winning fights had a 6:04 median and
5:14-7:35 10th-90th percentile. 510/570 winning fights
(89.5%) fell within 5-8 minutes. These are synthetic timings;
they are not measurements of real Discord players.

The pass covers all 86 catalog creatures at five levels (430 stat profiles),
9,736 same-rarity fights and 11,340 raid trials. The developer-only Ouroboros
is included in the stat sheet but excluded from competitive/raid conclusions.
All 85 playable species appear in the broad raid sweep and peer comparisons.
The complete roster is in balance-after/roster-overview.csv; detailed inputs
and outputs are in creature-stats.csv, duels.csv and raids.csv.

CONFIRMED PROBLEMS AND CHANGES

1. Gear used to increase raid HP because the boss scaled directly from current
   party attack. Its attack and defense also followed party HP and defense.
   This cancelled investment, and sometimes made an upgraded build slower.
   Budgets now use a fixed reference roster for the raid's level band and
   trainer count. Gear, training, bond and consumables cannot raise the budget.
   A regression checks that independence for every playable species.

2. A boss previously attacked one random trainer every eight seconds. Large
   parties divided that pressure among ten people. Normal volleys now hit
   every standing trainer. Boss-specific attack/defense/HP profiles preserve
   different encounters; solved patterns reduce pressure or grant protection.
   Missed bursts scale from boss attack and defender armor, rather than
   shrinking into trivial percentages of HP at high defense. Chronos echoes
   have a 12%-of-target-HP cap on their contribution-based extra damage.

3. The old two-second player recovery made click speed dominate raids. Speed
   now controls action recovery between 3.5 and 6.5 seconds around a five-second
   reference. Weave and Tend each retain their own eight-second support timer,
   so an attack recovery does not block a six-second pattern response.
   The displayed recovery changes with the active companion and slow effects.

4. Freeze teams could interrupt every boss turn. Freeze/sleep now interrupt a
   boss for one turn, with resistance preventing another interruption for the
   following two boss turns. Ordinary duel condition durations are unchanged;
   freeze skills cost 25 MP instead of 15 to curb their repeated use there.

5. Percentage-of-enemy-HP passives and damage over time ignored normal stat
   budgets. Corrupted Dragon's old 15% extra HP damage let the level-30,
   three-trainer, four-second-input stress team finish six bosses in an average
   12.7 seconds. Void Flame is now 6% in ordinary fights, capped at 20% of
   attacker ATK per hit against bosses. Corrupted Leviathan remains a 3% drain
   in ordinary fights, capped at 12% ATK against bosses. Its drain now counts
   toward raid damage contribution and cannot fire from a defeated actor.
   Boss burn/poison/blight ticks cap at 75%/50%/65% of the applying actor's ATK,
   including its damage multiplier when the condition is applied.

6. Several long-fight passives compounded without bounds. Zodiac now adds
   5% of starting ATK/SPD per action, capped at +40%. Ascended Pegasus adds
   8% of starting SPD, capped at +100%; reaching double speed really unlocks
   resistance bypass. Ancient Abyss erosion has an 85% starting-stat floor.
   Radiant Kitsune's extra strike is 1.5x every third action, replacing 9x.
   These remain useful identities without exponential damage or stat collapse.

7. Ancient Genesis bypassed every point of armor. It now pierces 35% and heals
   10% of actual damage. Ancient Chronos's extra-basic chance is 40% instead
   of 30%. Terminus's status-inflicting ultimate uses 2.2x power instead of
   1.4x, compared with the normal 2.5x signature. Catalog descriptions match.
   Percentage fields now consistently use percentage points, including a
   regression for the intended 30% Null Origin armor pierce. This preserves
   that passive's original intended calculation; it is not a new 100x buff.

8. Basic/skill/guard actions now rebuild one ultimate charge in the shared
   resolver. Bonus strikes do not add extra charge; signatures spend all three.
   NPCs no longer receive free full charge merely for having 50 mana. Wild
   enemies and raid companions start at zero charges. Raid mana follows the
   shared up-to-40% starting allowance instead of a hard 25 MP reset; the
   equipped Genesis Spark perk can add 20 MP. AI avoids known condition
   immunities rather than repeatedly wasting the same skill into them.

9. Three species had less than the 50 maximum mana needed for a signature at
   level 1; another three could fall below 50 with a mana-reducing disposition.
   Barkley, Goblin, Slime, Centry, Mudpup and Manticore now have 55 base mana,
   sufficient even with the -10% disposition. All 86 species pass the check
   under every disposition. Hydra and Radiant Hydra's regeneration skills
   now heal 20% and cleanse for 15 MP, instead of damaging and slowing an enemy.
   Ouroboros Ring's description now matches its existing 18% lifesteal/5% HP.

10. Starter base budgets were uneven. Changes preserve their different roles:
    Species       HP        ATK       DEF       SPD       MP
    Prismite      85->90    35->40    50        45        70
    Twine         70->85    40->45    30->35    70->80    65
    Gloop        105->110   30->35    40->45    35        60
    Barkley      115->110   40->35    55        20        35->55
    Their four earned awakenings retain distinct forms and passives.

11. Higher-level raids formerly paid the same rewards. Gold, shards, tokens,
    guaranteed materials, trainer EXP and companion EXP now scale from 1x
    at level 10 to 2x at level 50. Random bonus loot is still one item;
    catch odds and eight/twelve-clear guarantees are unchanged. The tier is
    journaled with victories, so interrupted settlements replay the same
    payout and cannot pay twice.

RAID BUDGET AND TARGET DEFINITION

Each trainer's highest companion level includes all three bench slots. The
median across trainers is rounded down to a five-level band and clamped to
10-50. This is a group difficulty rule: one high-level trainer can help a
lower-level group. It is not a separate adaptive budget for each participant.

Reference rosters: levels 10-14 Griffin/Basilisk/Wyvern; 15-24 Sphinx/
Thunderbird/Yeti; 25-39 Dragon/Kraken/Fenrir; 40-50 Atlas/Ascended Phoenix/
Nebuloom. Reference rarity growth, boss profiles and fixed tier/mana-rotation
factors set HP, ATK and DEF. HP scales approximately with trainers^0.95 and
a seven-minute reference damage budget. Actual builds can finish sooner or
later; there is no artificial minimum fight duration. The ten-minute limit
remains. Raid type advantage/resistance is 1.5x/0.75x, compared with 2x/0.5x
in normal duels, so unfavorable raid matchups are less punitive.

Actual live boss budgets at level 30 with three trainers:

ancient_chronos          24,458     227     133

Damage begins with ATK * move power * 100/(100 + effective DEF), then applies
type, crit, focus, gear, conditions, passives and the 0.85-1.15 damage roll.
The shared hit resolver applies evasion, guard, shields, reduction and survival.
Basic attacks restore 10 MP; the standard ultimate uses 2.5x power and 50 MP.
The stat CSV records all creature inputs and sampled neutral-target damage.
Unused fixed boss stat stubs were removed from the command definitions; caught
companion base stats and the live raid boss budget are separate game roles.

Boss/level HP adjustments address the long tails measured in calibration:
early Corrupted Leviathan/Fenrir, mid-tier Ancient encounters, and level-30
Leviathan. These depend on fixed reference tiers, never current gear or ATK.

Broad stress sweep: all 85 species, levels 10/30, 1/3/10 trainers, 2/4/6-second
inputs, all six bosses, no gear and three identical companions per trainer.
The same scenario matrix was run before and after. This deliberately sends
common companions into high-tier raids and sends earned Ancient companions
into low-tier raids. Its aggregate winning median is not the pacing target.

Input gap    Old wins Old time  New wins New time
2 seconds      100.0%     0:50     54.6%     4:04
4 seconds       99.2%     1:44     54.5%     4:08
6 seconds       96.2%     2:36     51.6%     4:30

The reduced aggregate win rate is expected in this adversarial matrix:
commons and weak homogeneous teams no longer clear almost every level-30
raid, while earned high-rarity teams can still beat lower tiers quickly.
Full outcomes and remaining boss HP are retained; failures are not dropped.

Behavior trials (24 each, level 30, three prepared trainers): four-second
inputs won all trials with and without pattern responses. Missing a pattern
adds pressure but is recoverable for this geared cohort; this pass does not
establish that Weave is mandatory. Eight-second inputs won none under the
ten-minute limit. The game expects active play; this is not an idle-raid mode.
The model submits one support/combat input per cadence, although real support
buttons have independent recovery and expert players may combine them faster.

DUEL EVIDENCE

Same-rarity round robins include mirrors, levels 1/15/30/50, and initial
charge 0 or 3 across two deterministic seeds. Optimal AI chooses healing,
conditions, guards and signatures. These are diagnostics, not ranked ratings:
the small seed count, fixed move policy and speed tie order affect results.
Earned Radiant/Ascended forms are allowed to outperform their base peers.

The 20-round judge rate fell from 3.6% to
2.1%. Level-30 Ancient Chronos went from 0% peer
wins to 50%; Genesis and Abyss now each show 66.7%. The four base starters'
level-30 peer wins are 57.7% Prismite, 53.8% Twine, 73.1% Gloop and 61.5%
Barkley, compared with Barkley's previous 92.3%. This removes obvious extremes
without forcing every creature into the same role or identical win rate.
